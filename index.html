16-hour battery

A full week of commuting or a 250 km weekend ride on a single charge. USB-C fast charging takes you from empty to 80% in 45 minutes.

IPX7 waterproof

Sealed against driving rain, road spray, and pressure washing. If your ride continues in a storm, so does VeloGuard.

Adaptive smart light

The 65-lumen tail light reads the radar: steady in clear road, rapid flash when a vehicle closes in — exactly when drivers need to notice you most.
